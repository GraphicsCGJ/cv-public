Publications

Journal Article

Accurate and Stable Alignment of Virtual and Real Spaces Using Consumer-Grade Trackers

J. An, G. Choi, W. Chun, Y. Joo, S. Park, I. Ihm.

Virtual Reality (Springer), vol. 26, no. 1, pp. 125–141, March 2022.

Abstract: Sharing a physical space across VR/AR/MR environments requires aligning the coordinate systems of the physical and virtual worlds accurately and stably without expensive professional hardware. Using a custom calibration board and consumer-grade position trackers, this work establishes a robust physical reference frame and estimates its exact geometric relationship to virtual space, ensuring high spatial accuracy and stability for multi-user collaborative XR environments.

Conference Proceeding

On Sharing Physical Geometric Space between Augmented and Virtual Reality Environments

W. Chun, G. Choi, J. An, W. Seo, S. Park, I. Ihm.

2019 IEEE Conference on Virtual Reality and 3D User Interfaces (IEEE VR), pp. 884–885, Osaka, Japan, March 2019. (Poster)

Abstract: AR and VR systems operate in isolated, independent coordinate systems, presenting challenges for multi-user collaboration in shared environments. This work proposes a methodology to estimate the geometric transform matrix between AR and VR world coordinates using a specialized tracking board, enabling real-time spatial collaboration within a single, unified physical room.

M.S. Thesis

An Effective Estimation of Coordinate System in Real Space Using Hybrid Marker Image

Gyujin Choi

M.S. thesis, Sogang University, 2019 (Advisor: Prof. Insung Ihm).

Abstract: Traditional single-marker AR alignment suffers from tracking degradation when the user moves out of direct line of sight. This work proposes a multi-marker hybrid tracking framework utilizing local cameras and a hierarchy of recognition states to continuously estimate a shared coordinate system. Tracking output is stabilized using Nearest-Neighbor/Gaussian interpolation and quaternion orientation averaging, and validated on Microsoft HoloLens and Samsung Galaxy devices, providing superior mobility and alignment stability.
