# Publications

## Journal Articles

**Accurate and Stable Alignment of Virtual and Real Spaces Using Consumer-Grade Trackers**
J. An, **G. Choi**, W. Chun, Y. Joo, S. Park, I. Ihm.
*Virtual Reality* (Springer), vol. 26, no. 1, pp. 125–141, March 2022.
[:material-link: doi.org/10.1007/s10055-021-00542-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10055-021-00542-1)

> Sharing a physical space across VR/AR/MR requires accurately and stably aligning the coordinate systems of the real and virtual worlds — ideally without expensive dedicated equipment. Using a purpose-built calibration board and three consumer-grade position trackers, the work establishes a reference frame in physical space and estimates its geometric relationship to virtual space with high numerical accuracy and stability, enabling users of different XR technologies to collaborate in one shared space.

## Conference Proceedings

**On Sharing Physical Geometric Space between Augmented and Virtual Reality Environments**
W. Chun, **G. Choi**, J. An, W. Seo, S. Park, I. Ihm.
*2019 IEEE Conference on Virtual Reality and 3D User Interfaces (IEEE VR)*, pp. 884–885, Osaka, Japan, March 2019. *(Poster)*
[:material-link: ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8798228](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8798228)

> AR and VR each operate in independent coordinate systems, making it hard for users of the two environments to share one physical space and collaborate. The work proposes estimating the geometric transform between the AR and VR world spaces using a purpose-designed tracking board, so that AR and VR users can collaborate within a single unified physical environment.

## Thesis

**An Effective Estimation of Coordinate System in Real Space Using Hybrid Marker Image**
M.S. thesis, Sogang University, 2019 (advisor: Prof. Insung Ihm).

> Single-marker AR alignment breaks down as the user moves, forcing them to keep staring at the marker. This work uses a *hybrid* marker image (Vuforia-based): depending on the user's position and gaze, only the relevant sub-marker is recognised to estimate the shared coordinate system, with marker sizing derived from the camera intrinsic matrix and stabilisation via nearest-neighbour / Gaussian interpolation and quaternion averaging. Validated on Microsoft HoloLens and Samsung Galaxy Tab S6, improving movement freedom and alignment stability over single-marker approaches.
